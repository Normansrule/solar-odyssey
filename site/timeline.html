Dawn 1839 to 1900

Scientists notice that light can change the electricity in a material, and even make it. Nobody can yet say why: the electron has not been discovered.

    Morning 1900 to 1970

    Quantum physics explains the effect, chemists learn to grow perfect crystals, and Bell Labs builds the first solar cell good enough to power something useful, first in orbit.

      Midday 1970 to today

      Oil shocks, laws and factories turn a space technology into the cheapest electricity many countries have ever had.

        Afternoon the research frontier

        The day is not over. Silicon is close to its limit, so the frontier is about stacking materials, and proving the new ones last.
