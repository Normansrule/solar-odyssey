For teachers

Solar Odyssey is free to use in class and free to adapt. This page collects what you need to plan with it: what each lesson teaches, how long it takes, and a question bank you can print with or without answers.

Using the course in class

  • Order. The lessons build on each other: the story, how a cell works, solar today, the frontier, then the other renewables, storage and the grid. Every page also stands on its own, so you can pick single lessons.
  • Two depths. Pages open at the Intro level, written for high-school students with no physics background. The Expert switch in the header adds derivations and detail for advanced or university students. Both levels sit on the same page, so a mixed class can work side by side.
  • Each lesson ends with three to five check-your-understanding questions. They are checked in the student's browser and remembered only on that device.
  • No accounts, no tracking. The site collects nothing about students, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites, so it fits strict school privacy rules.
  • No internet in the room? Install the offline desktop app, or copy the site folder to a USB stick and open it with any browser (see About).
  • Reuse. Text and drawings are licensed CC BY 4.0: copy, translate, cut and adapt them for your class with a credit to Solar Odyssey. Numbers come with their sources on the Sources page, and fast-moving ones carry the date they were checked.
