How this course works

Every number has a source and a date
Each figure links to the paper, dataset or report it comes from, and the sources page says when we last checked it. Numbers that change quickly, like efficiency records and prices, are gathered on the State of the field page.
You learn by changing things
Most lessons have a widget: move a slider, build a circuit, pick a place on the map, and watch the physics answer. The equations behind them are checked by automated tests.
Two depths
Intro is written for anyone who has finished school science. Switch to Expert at the top of any page to add the derivations and the detail a specialist would want.
Works offline, and nothing is tracked
There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or third-party requests. Your progress stays in this browser. A desktop app runs the whole course without an internet connection; see About, privacy and download.
